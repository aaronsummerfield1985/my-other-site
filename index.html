Summer Circle

With Summer Circle, guitarist and composer Aaron Summerfield presents a suite of works that balance improvisational depth with a compelling melodic accessibility.

Recorded in an intimate setting that prioritizes natural resonance, the album serves as a meditation on cyclical time and the rituals of seasonal change.
